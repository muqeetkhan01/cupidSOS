First, we do not see the question “Do you follow any religion?” in the onboarding.

Second, we noticed the question “How tall are you?” uses two different measurement systems. You show feet and centimeters in the same place. In the Preference section you use feet, but in the Personal Profile section you use centimeters. This is very confusing.

Our suggestion is that users choose either feet or centimeters, and the app should keep the same measurement throughout the entire app.

Third, we want users to upload at least three photos before they can move to the next step.

Fourth, photo verification should be the final step of onboarding, and it must be completed before the user finishes onboarding.

Fifth, we noticed AI assistance or hints that help users answer Quirk questions and voice prompts. We think this is a good feature.
